Discord Integration

Link your Discord account to receive email notifications in the #email-notifier channel when any of your characters receive an email.

How to find your Discord User ID
  1. Open Discord and go to Settings → Advanced
  2. Enable "Developer Mode"
  3. Right-click your profile picture or username
  4. Select "Copy User ID"
