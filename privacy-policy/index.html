Privacy Policy
Last updated July 2, 2026. This page explains how Site-89 collects, uses, stores, and protects information.

This policy explains how Site-89 handles information when you browse the site, create an account, submit content, use community features, or contact the team. It is meant to be readable first and formal enough to be useful, and it may be updated as the project changes.

What We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, Site-89 may collect:

  • Account information such as usernames, email addresses, and authentication details.
  • Content you submit, including feedback, forum posts, support messages, uploads, and other community contributions.
  • Technical and usage data such as page visits, browser type, device information, and session or analytics data.
  • Information related to Discord-linked or Discord-referenced features, if you connect or interact with them.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to run the site, authenticate accounts, moderate content, respond to requests, improve site features, understand how the site is used, and keep community tools operating safely.

Who Can Access It

Access to personal information is limited to Site-89 staff and administrators who need it to operate, support, or moderate the site. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

Third-Party Services

Some features may depend on third-party services such as hosting, analytics, authentication, forms, or Discord-related tools. Those services may process data according to their own policies. Site-89 is not responsible for how those services operate outside of our control.

Cookies And Analytics

Site-89 may use cookies, session storage, or similar technologies to keep the site working properly and to understand basic usage patterns. We may also use analytics or logging tools to help us monitor performance and security.

Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the site, maintain accounts, support moderation, preserve community history, or meet administrative and security needs. Retention periods may vary depending on the data type and the reason it was collected.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect your account credentials and report suspicious activity if you notice it.

Children And Age

Site-89 is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements that apply to the services we use, including any related Discord or platform requirements. If we learn that a user does not meet those requirements, we may restrict or remove access.

Your Choices

You may request help with account data, content removal, or other privacy questions by contacting the Site-89 team through the feedback page. We may need to verify requests before taking action.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The latest version will replace older versions once published here.

Contact

For questions about privacy, use the feedback page or another contact method provided by the site.
