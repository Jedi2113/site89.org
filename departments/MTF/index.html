Mobile Task Force Red-Four is a specialized quick-reaction force composed of elite operatives from Mobile Task Force Unit Nu-7 "Hammer Down." Stationed in close proximity to Site-89, Red-Four serves as an immediate response asset for external threats, anomaly containment breaches, and high-risk tactical operations that exceed the capabilities of standard Site security.

Service in Red-Four - For Tactical Operatives

Red-Four recruitment focuses on experienced personnel with strong tactical backgrounds, disciplined training, and proven capability under pressure. Because Red-Four is only a squadron-sized detachment rather than a full MTF branch, openings are rare and selection standards are intentionally strict.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Rapid deployment in response to external threats and containment breaches
  • Tactical support for high-risk anomaly interactions
  • Site-89 perimeter security and threat interdiction
  • Joint operations coordination with Site department leadership
  • Advanced anomaly containment and recovery procedures
  • Training and certification in Foundation combat protocols
  • After-action analysis and operational debriefing

Operational Requirements

Red-Four operates under strict military discipline, advanced training certifications, and high-level Foundation clearance. When openings do appear, applicants are generally expected to have some form of military or security background, whether from within the Foundation or outside it. Personnel must demonstrate:

  • Tactical proficiency and weapons certification
  • Advanced anomaly containment knowledge
  • Rapid decision-making under extreme stress
  • Team coordination and leadership capability
  • Psychological resilience and mental stability
  • Full compliance with MTF Nu-7 command structure
  • Commitment to Foundation security protocols
  • The maturity to meet the high standards expected of MTF personnel

Getting Started

Red-Four operates under joint authority from Site-89 and MTF Nu-7 command. Interested personnel should contact the MTF liaison office to discuss long-term eligibility, prior experience, and whether recruitment is currently open.

Interested in joining Red-Four?
Openings are uncommon. Applicants should expect a selective process and should already bring military, security, or equivalent tactical experience. Contact the MTF office at mtf.red4@site89.org

Formation Size: Squadron-level detachment

"Small unit. Fast response. High standards."

Red-Four is not large enough to be broken into multiple permanent operational units on-site. It functions as a single squadron-sized MTF presence, with personnel filling mission roles as needed under the direction of squadron leadership and Nu-7 command.

Role Coverage: Response, containment, security support, and tactical deployment
Recruitment: Rare openings only
Expectation: Applicants are held to a higher standard than ordinary security personnel
